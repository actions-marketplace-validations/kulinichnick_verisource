## 1. Core Brand Truth
Aurea Coffee Roasters roasts small-batch single-origin and signature specialty coffee to order, honoring historic Catholic and Orthodox saints and ancient Christian heritage. Every bag includes a scannable QR code linking directly to pour-over/espresso brew guides and the featured saint's biography.
